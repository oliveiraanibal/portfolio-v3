I'm Aníbal, a Senior UX Product Designer and certified Product Owner in Porto. That mix is why I care as much about what gets built, and in what order, as about how it looks.

What I do

I design products for complex, high-stakes work: fraud prevention at Feedzai, AI-assisted cataloguing for the National Library of the Netherlands, offer management for Shell, and smart-lighting platforms at Tridonic. Most of it is B2B, where the users are experts and a wrong click has a cost.
