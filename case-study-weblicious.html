Work / Project note

WebLicious

Nearly ten years running my own web studio: selling, scoping, designing and building for more than 50 clients in Portugal and the UK.

Company
WebLicious, my studio
When
2008–2018
My role
Founder: strategy, design, front-end, marketing
Team
Me and two contributors I mentored
Clients
Small businesses, UK studios, NHS, Sonae MC

In short

The opportunity

In 2008 most small businesses in Portugal had no website, and the crisis pushed them to look for cheaper ways to find customers.

What I did

Sold and scoped every project, designed and built the sites, ran the marketing, and kept doing NHS and other UK work from Portugal after almost two years in London.

The outcome

50+ clients over ten years

The regular accounts were sold to a competitor in 2018, so every client kept their service.

Key decisionWebsites as products, not quotes

Three packs with a starting price

Why

Each pack added one capability, from a small static site to one the client could edit, to a shop. A client knew what their budget bought before the first meeting.

Trade-off

Fixed packs leave money on the table for bigger clients, so anything beyond a pack was scoped on top: a starting point, not a ceiling.

Reflection. Scope is a product decision, and every sale starts with discovery: "I need a website" was never the real need. Both habits run through my product work today.
